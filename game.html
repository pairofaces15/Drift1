Bristol Bay Drift

How to play

Humpy $0.60/lbChum $0.60 Red $2.00King $2.50

RunDrag anywhere to steer, or switch to helm controls in Options for a throttle and wheel. She's heavy and slow to answer.

FishYou get paid by the pound, and the price is the whole game — a red is worth eight humpies. Humpies and chums are most of what you'll get. Reds and kings come through thin and fast — when one shows you have seconds to be across it.

ReadYou can't see the fish. Jumpers ring where one just came out. Birds lag the school and half of them sit on nothing. Most fish travel the rip. Run water to paint it on the sounder.

ThrowSET pays net off the drum while you're moving. Lay it across their path, then tow the running end while she soaks.

HaulHAUL puts you on deck. Hold DRUM to bring net over the roller — let go to pick.

PickManual, or switch to auto in Options and the crew works the web while you run the drum. Drag a fish out of the web. Clean gillers come in one pull; rolled ones take more. Anything that reaches the drum is rolled up and gone.

Make itHit the ticket and get at least one load on the tender. Fish still in your hold at the closure do not count. Every level after fishes harder than the last.

HoldYour hold depends on the boat you're running. Fill her and you fish nothing until you run to the tender and pump off.

FleetThree other drifters are working the same water and their gear takes the same fish. The finder shows your place in the fleet.

GroundSand bars lie across the district. Waves stand up and break over them, the sounder shows the bottom coming up, and she drags if you run onto one. Fish work round them.

TideShe drifts while you're picking. Cross the district line and your gear stops fishing. The opener runs sunny into the dark, and it comes on to blow after sundown.
